The public 104-second programme soundtrack combines this recorded firework
with original synthesized bass, echo, ember crackles and timed stereo placement.
The recording's explosion peak is aligned to each visual burst, not looped
independently of the show clock. The preview is preserved under audio/sources/
so scripts/create-footprint-soundscape.py can reproduce the mix offline.

Reference photograph is viewed for visual study only. No Olympic footage,
photographs, original music, emblems or logos are included in the programme.
